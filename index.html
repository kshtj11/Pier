How to use Pier

A prototype. The hats are scripted, not a live AI. Nothing is saved: a reload starts the example again.

Move around
Scroll, or swipe with two fingers. Or hold Space and drag.
Zoom
Ctrl/⌘ + scroll, or pinch on a trackpad.
Open a paper or note
Zoom into it, or double-click it.
Back to the board
Esc, or zoom out.
Read from a peek line
Click the quoted line on a faded paper. The reader opens at that line.
Make a quote
In the reader, select text, then Highlight.
Put a quote in a note
Drag the quote card onto the note.
New note
Double-click the empty board, or press N.
Ask a hat
? on a card (Bounce). H opens the Hats pane.
Why did a hat say that?
Right-click any hat line: Why are you saying this?
Your own PDFs
Ctrl/⌘ + O, or drop a PDF on the board.
Undo, redo
Ctrl/⌘ + Z, Ctrl/⌘ + Y.
Find papers, timeline
☰ at the top left opens the sidebar.
Writing room
Write to just write. Argue marks your claims and lets the hats question them.
Open this again with ? at the top right.
